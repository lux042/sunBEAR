# New York Times in sunBEAR 1.6.0

1. Close the previous app and open this version's sunBEAR.exe. Your existing Windows library is reused.
2. Choose **New York Times**, then **NYT sign in**. Sign in inside sunBEAR; your normal browser's login is separate.
3. Paste a topic/search/article URL, or choose **Search New York Times**, search in the embedded browser, and choose **Import this page** once results appear.
4. Start with one search page. Keep **Save article pages** checked to create readable offline HTML. Available article text is retained in the library even when that option is off.
5. If saving pauses for access, finish sign-in or check the article in the Browser tab, then choose **Continue after sign-in**. It rechecks the same article. **Stop task** retains completed work.
6. Select a search in the sidebar, inspect records and article details, and use **Export…** for TSV or EndNote.

For existing records use **Downloads / files > Download article pages**; **Read saved article** opens the local HTML copy. Offline HTML contains available text and citation information, not images, video or the exact original layout.

Searches without a query in their address can be imported from the visible browser results. Games links are omitted unless games, crosswords or puzzles are explicitly searched for. More pages means additional available result batches; it does not guarantee exhaustive coverage.

For historical material, use a date-filtered NYT search or **TimesMachine**, select an individual article, and import it. Whole issue viewers are not individual article records. PDFs are downloaded only when offered by the publisher and accessible to your account; webpages are not converted into PDFs.

Progress is indeterminate during discovery, then advances after each record is processed, including failures. Live account-dependent access and full article completeness need verification on your Windows installation.
